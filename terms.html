Game terms

Last updated: 4 November 2026

These are the terms of Plague Survival in plain words. The game is licensed to you under Apple's Standard EULA (on Google Play, under Google Play's terms); this page adds what is particular to the game. Nothing here takes away rights the law of your country gives you as a consumer.

Who we are

The game is sold by the developer of Plague Survival. Email coming soon.

Gems

  • Gems are the game's one currency you can buy with money. Gold, materials and wax are earned by playing and are never sold for money. You can swap a few gems for gold, at most 3 times a day (5 gems for 500 gold).
  • Every price in gems also shows about what it costs in your own money, worked out at the price of the smallest gem purse, so it never looks cheaper than it is.
  • Gems don't expire. We won't lower what your gems buy, or take back what you bought, except where a payment is refunded or reversed (see below).
  • Gems and the things they buy have no cash value and can't be sold, traded or moved to another person.
  • Nothing in the game that you can buy with money or gems is random: you always see exactly what you get before you pay. Chests found during a run are free rewards.

Buying

  • Apple (or Google) takes every payment and is the seller of record for it. Prices are the ones the store shows, with tax as the store applies it.
  • Before your first purchase with gems, the game asks you to tick that you want things you buy with gems delivered at once, and that you then lose the 14-day right to withdraw from that gem purchase. Gems you haven't spent keep the store's refund route.
  • Purchases on a child's account follow the controls a parent sets with Apple's Family Sharing and Screen Time (for example, Ask to Buy), or Google Play's parental controls.

Subscriptions

The Lantern Oath (monthly or yearly) and the Parish Subscription renew automatically at the price shown on their card until you cancel. A first-month offer is paid at the start and then renews at the full monthly price. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal, in the game (the card's MANAGE SUBSCRIPTION) or in your store account's subscriptions. A day's gems you don't claim are not paid later.

Refunds

Ask Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com (or Google Play's order history). When a store refunds a purchase, the game takes back what that purchase gave: its gems (your balance can go below zero until new gems cover it), its ledger tiers or its subscription days.

Ads

Most ads play only when you tap to watch one for a reward. One skippable advert may come after a finished run, never in your first two runs; Remove Ads turns it off. Ads are limited to general audiences with parental guidance at most. Your ad choices are in Settings → PRIVACY.

Changes

If these terms change, the date above changes. A change will never take away gems or things you already paid for.
