A horde roguelike for one thumb

Plague Survival is a horde roguelike for iPhone that you play with one thumb. Your weapons strike on their own, and at every level-up you choose what your hero becomes. Ten timed waves, a shop between each, and a boss at the end. Below ground wait five dungeons of sealed rooms, traps and relics: the first is free, and four more come in the Dungeons DLC. Dark fantasy in 32x32 pixel art, and it plays fully offline.
