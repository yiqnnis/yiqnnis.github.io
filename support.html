Questions

How do I restore my purchases?

In the game, open Settings → PURCHASES → Restore purchases, signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. Skins, bundles and badges come back. Gems and gold that were inside a bundle are paid once per player profile, so a restore gives back the skin or badge only. Gem purses are used up when spent and are not restored, but your balance travels with your cloud save.

How do subscriptions work, and how do I cancel?

There are three: the Lantern Oath (monthly, or yearly), and the Parish Subscription (monthly, which also opens each month's Parish Ledger page). Each renews at the price shown on its card until you cancel. A first-month offer, when the App Store shows you one, is paid at the start and then renews at the full monthly price. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews: in the game, open the card and tap MANAGE SUBSCRIPTION, or go to iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Gems you already claimed stay yours. The game terms explain the rest in plain words; the licence is Apple's Standard EULA.

How do I keep my progress on a new iPhone or iPad?

Sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings → Game Center) and turn on iCloud, with the same Apple Account on both devices. The game saves a copy of your progress there on its own, and Settings → CLOUD SAVE → Sync now syncs at once. Without Game Center the game still plays fully offline, but progress stays on that one device.

Why can't I see Game Center leaderboards?

Leaderboards need Game Center sign-in and a connection. While you are signed out, the board shows made-up guild rivals on this phone instead (they are not real players, and the board says so). Scores on Game Center leaderboards appear with your Game Center nickname.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Reminders are off until you say yes on the notice board. Turn them off any time in Settings → REMINDERS, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Plague Survival. The game sends at most one a day, never between 21:00 and 09:00, and only while the game is closed.

What is the anonymous play log?

A record of game events (runs, waves, purchases) kept on your phone to help improve the game. It holds no name, email or ad ID, and in this version it never leaves your phone. Switch it off in Settings → PRIVACY → Anonymous play log; that deletes it. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side. To remove everything:

  • On the phone: delete the app. That removes your progress, settings and the play log from that device.
  • In iCloud: in iOS Settings, tap your name → iCloud → Manage Storage (or Manage Account Storage), choose Plague Survival and delete its data. This removes the cloud save from all your devices. The wording can differ between iOS versions.
  • Game Center scores are kept by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; manage your Apple data at privacy.apple.com.

Purchases stay linked to your Apple Account, so you can restore skins and bundles if you reinstall later.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles every payment, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Gems you have not spent can be refunded that way. Things bought with gems are delivered at once, so before your first gem purchase the game asks you to agree that you lose the 14-day right to withdraw from it. See the game terms.
