Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Plague Survival ("the game") is made by the developer of Plague Survival ("we", "us"). Contact: coming soon

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You never sign up or log in with us, and we run no server of our own.
  • We never receive your name, email, contacts, location or any identifier for you.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show them. In the EU, the UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first, and on iPhone Apple asks whether you allow tracking (section 5).
  • Purchases and the Lantern Oath subscription are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details (section 4).
  • Cloud save and leaderboards use your own Game Center and iCloud account, if you are signed in (section 3).
  • The game's play log stays on your phone. Nothing is sent to us (section 1).
  • We do not sell or share your data, because we do not have any.

1. Data kept on your device

The game stores your progress, settings, purchases and run checkpoint in files on your device. They stay there unless you use cloud save (section 3). Deleting the game deletes them.

Anonymous play log. The game keeps a log of gameplay events on your device (for example "a run started", "wave 4 reached", "a purchase finished"), tagged with a random number made only for this log. It contains no name, email, account, advertising identifier, IP address, precise device model or location. In this version the log is not sent anywhere: the game has no analytics service connected, so the log never leaves your device and it is not copied into your cloud save. You can switch it off under Settings → PRIVACY → Anonymous play log; switching it off deletes the log and its random number at once.

2. No accounts

You never make an account with us and there is no login. The only sign-in the game uses is the one already on your phone: Game Center, which is optional (section 3). Everything in the game works without it.

3. Game Center and iCloud

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the game:

  • saves a copy of your progress (including the record of what you bought) to your own Game Center / iCloud account, so it can be restored on another device;
  • submits your scores to leaderboards, where they appear with your Game Center nickname and can be seen by other players.

This data is stored by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). We cannot read your saved games. How to delete this copy is on the support page.

4. Purchases and the Lantern Oath subscription

In-app purchases (gem purses, looks, bundles and Remove Ads) are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, email or payment details. The game checks each purchase on your device with Apple's StoreKit, receives a transaction identifier, the product bought and its date, and keeps them in your save file so a purchase is never delivered twice and can be restored.

The Lantern Oath is a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel it. For it, the game also keeps the start and end date of each paid month and which days you claimed your gems, so it knows when the Oath is active. You manage or cancel it in your Apple Account settings (the game's MANAGE SUBSCRIPTION button opens that page). Its terms are Apple's standard licence agreement (Apple's Standard EULA).

Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple refunds a purchase, the game may take back what that purchase gave.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Most ads play only when you tap a "watch an ad" button to earn a reward. One skippable advert may also appear after a finished run, never during play and never in your first two runs. Buying Remove Ads turns that advert off, and gives you every ad reward without watching.

To show, count and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and identifiers that only work inside this app;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location (country or city, not your exact place);
  • information about the ads you see and tap, and how you use the app around them;
  • crash and performance information about the ads.

Google decides how it uses this data (under EU law it is a separate "controller", not us). See "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services" (policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Your choices:

  • Consent (EU, UK, Switzerland): before any ad loads, the game shows Google's consent message. Personalised ads are shown only if you agree. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → PRIVACY → Privacy choices.
  • Tracking (iPhone): after that, Apple asks whether you allow the game to track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, the game never gets the advertising identifier. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • If you say no to either, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. Non-personalised ads still use some data, such as your IP address, to show the ad, count it and stop fraud. Saying no never changes anything else in the game.

Apart from Google's ad services, the game contains no third-party analytics or tracking code, and it connects to no server of ours. If we ever add another ad network or service that receives data, we will list it here first.

6. Reminders (notifications)

Reminders are off until you turn them on. If you say yes, the game asks iOS for permission and then schedules at most one local reminder a day on your device, never between 21:00 and 09:00. They are created on your phone; nothing is sent to us and no push service is used. Turn them off under Settings → REMINDERS, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Plague Survival.

7. Photos

If you tap SAVE on a run poster, the game asks permission to add an image to your photo library. It can only add images; it cannot see your photos. Nothing is uploaded.

8. Children

Plague Survival is a game for a general audience. Its App Store age rating is 9+. It is not directed at children and is not in the App Store's Kids category, but younger players may play it.

The game never asks for your birthday, and we collect no personal information from anyone, including children: no accounts, no chat, no server of our own. Before the first ads, the game asks your phone for your age range: on iPhone through Apple's Declared Age Range (iOS 26 or later), on Android through Google Play Age Signals. Apple or Google decide what to share and may ask you, or a parent, first; the game only learns a range such as "13 to 17", uses it for that launch, and stores no age. If the range says you are under 18, the game never asks to track you and asks Google for non-personalised ads only (Google's setting for users under the age of consent). If it says you are under 13, ads are also marked as directed at children and limited to general-audience ("G") content, and the anonymous play log never leaves the phone. Otherwise the game asks Google for ads rated for general audiences with parental guidance at most (Google's "PG" rating), never teen or mature ads. If your phone shares no range (an older iPhone, or you chose not to share), Google's consent message and Apple's tracking request decide, as for everyone.

Purchases, the subscription, Game Center and iCloud on a child's Apple Account follow the controls a parent sets through Apple's Family Sharing and Screen Time (for example, Ask to Buy). If you believe a child's personal information has reached us, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to look up, correct or delete. Your data on your device and in your Game Center / iCloud account is controlled as described above; for ad data, use your consent and tracking choices (section 5) and Google's tools at myadcenter.google.com. You can contact us with any privacy question, and you may complain to your local data protection authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority, dpa.gr).

10. Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new (for example, if the play log is ever sent off the device, or a new ad network is added), we will update this policy and the date above before that version is released.
